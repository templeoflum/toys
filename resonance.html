push a swing steadily and it settles into a swing of its own, matching your beat. push it at its natural frequency ω0 = √(k/m) — the rate it wants to swing on its own — and every push adds to the last: the motion builds. push off the beat and your pushes fight the swing, so it stays small. the steady swing size is A(ω) = F0 / √((k − mω²)² + (cω)²), and it peaks sharply right around ω0. that peak is resonance. weaker damping c makes the peak taller and narrower — less to bleed the built-up motion away.the driven damped harmonic oscillator · m·x″ + c·x′ + k·x = F₀cos(ωt) · A(ω) peaks at resonance, ω₀ = √(k/m) · field: driven oscillation
