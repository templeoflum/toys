bourdon gauge

pressure P
 
 
 
a curved metal tube with a flattened (oval) bore is closed at its free tip. raise the internal pressure and the flattened section tries to round out toward a circle — and a rounder section along a curve makes the curve open, so the C uncurls and its free tip travels. the tip travel is proportional to pressure over the working range; a linkage and rack-and-pinion turn that small tip motion into a needle sweep, so the needle angle = (P/Pmax)·sweep.the Bourdon tube pressure gauge · Eugène Bourdon 1849 · flattened curved tube uncurls under pressure, tip travel ∝ P, linkage drives the needle · field: elasticity / pressure measurement
