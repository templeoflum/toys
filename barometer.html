a tube closed at the top stands in a dish open to the air. the atmosphere presses on the dish and holds up a column of fluid; above it hangs a vacuum — the Torricelli void. the column stands exactly where its weight per area balances the pressure: h = P / (ρg). higher pressure → taller column. dense mercury needs only ~760 mm where water would need ~10.3 m.the mercury barometer · Evangelista Torricelli 1643 · h = P/(ρg), the atmosphere balances the column · field: fluid statics / atmospheric pressure
