press play and hold a moment — the tone climbs (or falls) without ever arriving; drag speed and hear the stair steepen. every voice is a plain sine an octave from its neighbors, sliding under a fixed loudness bell — what fades out the top re-enters the bottom. follow one voice to catch the loop in the act: the endlessness is the ensemble, not any voice. after roger shepard (1964); the effect is strongest in headphones and varies ear to ear — this toy plays the true mechanism, your ear supplies the illusion.
