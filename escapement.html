a swinging pendulum drives an anchor whose two pallets rock into a toothed escape wheel. each beat the anchor locks the wheel dead still, then releases exactly one tooth — the tooth slides off the pallet and gives the pendulum a tiny impulse back, replacing what friction stole. so the wheel does not spin freely: it steps one tooth per beat, chained to the swing — the tick-tock. the swing's period sets the rate: T = 2π√(L/g), longer pendulum, slower clock. give too little impulse and the swing dies; give enough and it holds forever.the escapement (verge / anchor) · Huygens' pendulum clock 1656 · one tooth per beat, T = 2π√(L/g) · field: horology / harmonic oscillator
