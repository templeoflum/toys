anorthoscope

slit
ghost
 
a star drifts past behind a narrow slit — at any instant only a thin vertical sliver of it shows. yet the eye stitches the slivers together across time into the whole star, seen as if all at once. widen the slit and the shape is simply there, no stitching needed; raise the ghost to lay the whole star faintly over the slit and check what you pieced together.
