shortest

 
the highlighted path is the shortest way from start to goal — not the shortest first step, but the least total length over the whole route. Dijkstra finds it by relaxing every edge: it grows a frontier outward from start, always settling the nearest unsettled node next, so once a node is settled its distance is final. remove a node or an edge on the best route and it must re-route — the next-best detour, always longer, never shorter. cut too much and start and goal fall into separate pieces: no path. reset restores the full graph.
