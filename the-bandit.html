the bandit

strategy
ε explore
 
 
a row of levers, each with a hidden payoff chance p—pull one and it pays 1 with probability p, else 0. you only see the observed rate (wins ÷ pulls), never the true p. so every pull is a bet two ways at once: exploit the lever that looks best so far, or explore an uncertain one to learn its real rate. greedy always pulls the current best—and can lock onto a lever that got lucky early, never trying the truly better one it under-sampled. ε-greedy pulls a random lever a fraction ε of the time; those explore-pulls cost reward now but find the best lever. regret is what that best lever would have paid minus what you actually earned—it only grows.the multi-armed bandit · explore vs exploit · ε-greedy (Watkins 1989; Sutton & Barto) · regret = Σ(p* − pchosen) · field: reinforcement learning / cognitive search
