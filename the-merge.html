three lanes fold into one at a merge point — a shared resource that serves only one at a time. its service rate is a fixed ceiling; feed in more than it can pass and the surplus backs up in the lanes. the discipline decides who pays: greedy always serves one priority lane first, so under overload a starved lane's backlog runs away — unfair; zipper strictly alternates, one from each lane in turn, so every lane gets an equal share and the piles stay balanced — fair. same ceiling, same equal arrivals, different fairness. the shared point made visible.the zipper merge · mutual exclusion on a shared resource · throughput vs. fairness
