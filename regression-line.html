regression line

 
a scatter of points. click empty plane to add one, drag one to move it, click a point to remove it. the line y = a + b·x is fitted by ordinary least squares — it sits where the sum of the squared vertical misses is as small as it can be. each miss is drawn as a vertical residual; its square is a literal square whose area is that miss squared. move the line off the fit and the total area only grows.ordinary least squares · b = Σ(x−x̄)(y−ȳ) / Σ(x−x̄)² · a = ȳ − b·x̄ · SSE = Σ(y−(a+b·x))²
