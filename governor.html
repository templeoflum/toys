two heavy flyballs hang from a spinning vertical spindle on hinged arms. spin it faster and the balls fly outward and rise, swinging up to the angle where gravity and the whirl balance: cosθ = g / (ω²L). the vertical drop below the pivot, h = g/ω², shrinks as speed rises. the rising balls lift a collar that closes a throttle — more speed cuts more fuel, so the engine holds one steady speed. negative feedback.the centrifugal (Watt) governor · James Watt & Matthew Boulton 1788 · cosθ = g/(ω²L), rising balls close the throttle · field: control / feedback governors
