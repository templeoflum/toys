raise the density and a traffic jam forms with no crash and no bottleneck — just cars, delay, and density; the jam then rolls backward against the traffic. at low density it flows free; cross the critical density and uniform flow breaks down into stop-and-go from nothing.phantom traffic jam · Nagel–Schreckenberg 1992 · Sugiyama et al. 2008 (22 cars, a ring, a jam from nothing)
