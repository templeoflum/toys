a sprung mass rides a bumpy road on a spring and a damper. the spring alone would let it ring — bounce on after every bump — so the damper bleeds that ringing into heat. how much it bleeds is set by one number, the damping ratio ζ: below one the mass overshoots and oscillates (a bouncy car), at one it settles fastest with no overshoot (the ideal), above one it is sluggish — no bounce but slow to return. watch the smooth mass trace filter the jagged road: the shock absorbed.damped driven oscillator · suspension · ζ = c / (2√(k·m))
