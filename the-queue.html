the queue

arrivals λ
service μ
 
arrivals come at rate λ, one server clears them at rate μ. their ratio ρ = λ/μ is the whole story: below 1 the line settles; as it nears 1 the wait explodes (L = ρ/(1−ρ)); at 1 and above the server can never catch up and the line grows forever. the town’s own dispatch — papers arriving faster than they’re signed.M/M/1 queue · Kendall 1953
