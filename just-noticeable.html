plot
 
two gray patches: the left is the baseline I, the right is I plus the step dI. raise dI until you can just tell them apart — that step is the just-noticeable difference. now raise the baseline: the same dI vanishes, and to feel it again you must grow the step. the just-noticeable step is a constant fraction of the baseline, dI = k·I with k = 0.10 — so equal ratios feel equal, and sensation runs logarithmically. raise the plot to draw the JND line, slope k through the origin. the eye is the judge; the k·I line is only the model's guess at where your eye lands.
