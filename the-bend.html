set a start and a heading — the direction you set off “straight,” never turning. on the globe that traces a great circle: the one circle whose plane cuts through the sphere’s centre, and it loops all the way back to where you began. press walk it to travel it — you feel yourself going dead straight, yet the path bends around the ball. now read the same path on the flat map beside it: the straight-on-the-sphere line comes out as a curve. nothing steered it; the surface did. a geodesic is the straightest a path can be on its surface (it turns nowhere) — on a sphere that is always a great circle, and flattening is what makes it look bent.
