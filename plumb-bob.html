a weighted bob hangs on a line from a pivot. gravity pulls the line to true vertical — straight down — whatever the wall behind it does. lean the frame by α and the line does not follow it: the line stays plumb and the wall swings away from it. the wedge between the still line and the frame edge is the wall's lean, and it equals exactly the tilt you dialled in. the line seeks the fixed reference; you read the wall's deviation from it.the plumb bob · masons of antiquity · gravity holds the line at true vertical, lean = frame tilt · field: statics / gravimetry
