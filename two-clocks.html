two clocks

 
two clocks run in every story. story-time is the order events happened; telling-time is the order — and the room — they get told in. the ribbons are the map between them. a device is a shape of that map: hold them level (scene), squeeze a long stretch into a moment (summary), open a moment wide (stretch), stop the story clock while the telling runs on (pause), or double the telling back to an earlier event (flashback — the ribbons cross). event names are gibberish on purpose — only the shape carries the idea.
