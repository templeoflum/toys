rest your eyes on the mark at the centre. the rings are dead still — only the whole pattern gently breathes in and out. yet the two rings seem to rotate in opposite directions. it is the slant of each little tile that hijacks your motion detectors as the pattern scales. set tilt to zero and, with the same zoom, the turning stops — the motion lived only in the slant.
