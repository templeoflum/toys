scintillate
 
the grey smudges haunting the crossings are not on the page — look straight at any one and it vanishes. where the pale streets meet, your retina's centre-surround cells are flanked by light on all four sides and dim the spot, so a ghost darkens every crossing but the one you fixate. drop the contrast to nothing and the grid (and its ghosts) dissolve; raise scintillate for the fiercer white-dot version, where the spots flick black in the corner of your eye.
