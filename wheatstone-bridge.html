a diamond of four resistors with a battery across one diagonal and a galvanometer across the other. you turn the balancing R3 until the galvanometer reads zero — the null. at that null no current crosses the bridge and the reading no longer depends on the battery or the meter, only the ratio: Rx = R1·R3/R2. a null instrument measures a balance, not a deflection.the Wheatstone bridge · Samuel Hunter Christie 1833, popularized by Charles Wheatstone 1843 · balance at R1/R2 = Rx/R3; measured at the null
