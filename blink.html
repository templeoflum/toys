a brief, soft, rhythmic blink rides the same suppression that hides your own blinks — you stop seeing it. but the catch: a change made under the blink is missed (change-blindness), so a commit-blink is a heartbeat, an aliveness signal — never the way a real change is announced.blink-suppression & change-blindness · visual perception (Volkmann; Rensink, O’Regan & Clark 1997)
