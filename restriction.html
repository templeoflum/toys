flow funnels toward a narrowing throat, and the throat's width caps how much can pass — the tightest point sets the ceiling for the whole line. feed in more than the throat can carry and the surplus has nowhere to go: it backs up upstream as a growing pile. throughput holds at the cap no matter how hard you push; the cost of the restriction shows as the backlog. pinch the width down and the pile grows faster; open it and the pile drains. the bottleneck made visible.flow restriction & the bottleneck · conservation of flow · throughput = min(input, capacity)
