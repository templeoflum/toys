two families of parallel rails run across one ground plane. each family — every rail in it heading the same direction — converges to a single vanishing point. drag set A or set B to swing a family's direction; its vanishing point slides along the horizon and no further. same direction, one point. the horizon is where the plane's parallels-at-infinity all land: the image of the line at infinity.
