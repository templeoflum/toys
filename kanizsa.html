three notched discs face inward and a bright white triangle springs up between them — with crisp edges and corners, floating over the discs, though no triangle is drawn. your eye completes the shape the notches imply. turn the discs so the notches face outward and the triangle vanishes — nothing was ever there; or raise the edge to trace where you see a border and find only paper along it.
