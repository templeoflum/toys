an unknown weight hangs at a fixed distance on the left; you slide a known counterpoise along the right arm until the beam sits level. at the null the two torques are equal — and the counterpoise's position reads the unknown mass straight off, no spring, no scale of force. weigh anything by moving a weight to where the lever agrees.the balance & steelyard · the equal-arm balance (ancient Egypt) · the Roman steelyard — weight by torque at the null, independent of gravity's strength
