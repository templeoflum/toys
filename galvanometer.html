a current through a coil of N turns and area A, sitting in a radial field B, feels a torque τ = N·I·A·B that twists it against a restoring spring k. at balance the needle rests where the two match, so the deflection θ is exactly proportional to the current: double the current, double the angle; reverse it, and the needle swings the other way. the sensitivity knob raises the field (or the turns), so the same current deflects further.the moving-coil galvanometer · d'Arsonval/Deprez 1882 · deflection θ ∝ current (τ = NIAB vs spring) · field: electromagnetism
