a sealed glass vial is curved in a broad arc and nearly full of fluid, with one small bubble. lighter than the fluid, the bubble floats to the highest point of the arc. level the vial and the high point is the centre — bubble between the graduations. tilt it by α and the high point shifts, so the bubble slides toward the raised end by x = R·α: a flatter vial (bigger radius R) moves the bubble more per degree, a more sensitive level.the spirit (bubble) level · Melchisedech Thévenot ~1661 · the bubble rises to the high point, x = R·tilt · field: statics / leveling
