micrometer

thimble
pitch p
divisions N
 
a precision screw of pitch p drives the spindle, and the thimble is cut into N divisions, so turning it by ONE division advances the spindle by only p/N. the fine thread amplifies: a large rotation of the thimble becomes a tiny, exactly-readable advance — that amplification is the whole idea. reading = barrel (mm, from full & half turns) + thimble (division × p/N).the micrometer screw gauge · William Gascoigne ~1638 — one thimble division = pitch/N; the screw amplifies rotation into a fine advance.
