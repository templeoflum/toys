a steady push meets a drag that grows with speed, so the object can only speed up until the two balance — then it holds a terminal velocity, a ceiling no extra time can beat. in air the drag grows as speed squared, so the ceiling rises only as the square root of the push: quadruple the push and the speed merely doubles.drag & terminal velocity · Stokes 1851 (linear) · Newton (quadratic, v²)
