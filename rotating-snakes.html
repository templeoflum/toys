nothing here moves — it is a still picture. the rings seem to creep and rotate because each little tile steps black→dark→white→light in one direction, and that lopsided run of brightness fools your motion detectors, most of all out of the corner of your eye. drop the asymmetry so the steps run even, and the drift dies though the picture barely changes; raise fixate for a dot to stare at and feel the motion settle where you look.
