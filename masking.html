press play, hear the thin steady tone at 1000 hz, then steer the louder band toward it — somewhere on the approach the tone drops out of the world, though its own level never moved; steer past and it returns. try the two sides: a band parked just BELOW 1000 hz hides it harder than one just above — masking spreads upward, low hides high. this is wegel & lane's 1924 measurement and the experiment behind fletcher's critical band (1940) — and the reason mp3 files are small: coders spend no bits on what the masker already hides. the drawn skirt is a schematic guide; the real threshold lives in your ears.
