walk
 
the monomyth is a ring of twelve stations — depart, be initiated, return. two thresholds cut it: crossing out into the special world near the top-right, crossing back near the bottom-left. pick a tale and drag walk to send it clockwise around the wheel; each beat lights the station it lands on. it is one lens — a template many tales fit, not a law that all stories must.the monomyth · Campbell 1949 gave the three arcs (departure · initiation · return); the popular twelve stations are Vogler's 1992 restatement · field: comparative mythology
