a cord runs from a pull-handle on the left to a fixed anchor on the right, hanging with slack. as you pull the left end the slack takes up first — the sag straightens and the anchor feels nothing; only once the slack is fully spent does the line go taut and force transmits to the far end. slack is a buffer: it absorbs the give before force carries through. a tow-rope, a coupling, a safety line.slack & taut · the give before force transmits · a mechanical buffer (tow-rope, coupling)
