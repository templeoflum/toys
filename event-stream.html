a memoryless stream has no memory — a long silence is rare but never due; how long you’ve waited tells you nothing about when the next event comes. the histogram of gaps falls off as an exponential, and that shape is the memorylessness. flip to clockwork to feel the difference: there, waiting really does make the next arrival overdue.Poisson point process · exponential inter-arrivals, memoryless · Palm 1943, Khinchin 1955, Kingman 1993
