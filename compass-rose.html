a true-north ring and a magnetic-north ring share one center, offset by the local declination — the angle between where the needle points and true north. read the fixed lubber line against each ring and the two bearings differ by exactly that offset: magnetic = true − declination (east positive, west negative). where declination is zero the rings coincide and the compass points true.magnetic declination (variation) · magnetic bearing = true − declination · Halley's 1701 Atlantic chart (first isogonic lines) · agonic line = zero declination
