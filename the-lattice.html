the lattice

the divisors of 12, ordered by "divides". ∧ meet   ∨ join
click two elements.
every pair of elements has a single meet (their greatest lower bound — here the largest number dividing both) and a single join (least upper bound — the smallest both divide into). that both always exist, and are unique, is what makes this order a lattice — the structure the whole house is named for. click any two nodes; their meet and join light up. click a third to start over.
