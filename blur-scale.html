blur-scale

blur σ
 
the signal carries features at three widths — a slow swell, a middle ripple, and two sharp spikes. raise σ to blur it by degrees. blurring is a low-pass: a feature of width w is erased once σ grows to about w. so the spikes wash out first, the ripple next, and the slow swell survives to the end — a scale keeps only what is broader than itself.
