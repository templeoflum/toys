deform
 
drag the peg. the winding number counts how many times the loop circles it — and it is a whole number no stretching can change: switch the shape, crank deform, wobble it into a mess, the count holds. the only way to move it is to drag the peg across the loop’s edge. the dial on the right shows why: it traces the direction from peg to loop, and that arrow wraps the dial exactly that many times.
