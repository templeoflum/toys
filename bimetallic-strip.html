two metals with different thermal expansion bonded into one strip: heat the pair and the higher-expansion metal grows more, so the strip curves toward the slower side — the more heat, the tighter the bend. curvature grows with the coefficient mismatch and the temperature alike; the tip swings farther as the strip is longer, by the square of its length. a pointer on the tip reads temperature off the bend — the thermostat.the bimetallic strip · John Harrison ~1759 (gridiron) · Breguet · curvature ∝ temperature change · field: thermal expansion
