a rack cut into the plunger drives a pinion, geared through to the pointer. the train amplifies: a tiny linear plunger travel becomes a large needle sweep. the ratio is fixed — 1 needle revolution = 1 mm of plunger travel, and the face carries 100 divisions, so 1 division = 0.01 mm. reading = whole revolutions (the small counter) + needle division ÷ 100. that reconstruction is the built-in check: it must equal the plunger travel.the dial indicator / dial test gauge · a rack-and-pinion gear train amplifies plunger travel into needle sweep · 1 rev = 1 mm, 1 division = 0.01 mm · field: precision metrology / mechanical amplification
