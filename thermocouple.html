couple
 
 
two dissimilar metals joined at a hot junction; their free ends meet again at a cold (reference) junction. the Seebeck effect makes each metal carry a voltage per degree, and the two differ — so the pair produces an EMF from the temperature difference: V = S·(T_h − T_c). it reads the difference, not the absolute heat — when the two junctions sit at the same temperature the voltage is zero, however hot they both are. a different couple gives a steeper or shallower volts-per-degree.the thermocouple · Thomas Seebeck 1821 · V = S·ΔT, EMF from a temperature difference · field: thermoelectricity
