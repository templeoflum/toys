every gray line here is dead horizontal and perfectly parallel — only the tiles slide. shift alternate rows by a half-tile and your edge-detectors, reading the offset light/dark corners against the gray mortar, infer a tiny tilt at each corner; the tilts sum along the line into a wedge that is not there. drop offset to zero and the lines snap straight; starve the mortar to nothing and the cue that feeds the tilt dies.
