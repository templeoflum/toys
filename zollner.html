every long line is vertical and perfectly parallel to its neighbours. the short cross-strokes lean one way on one line, the other way on the next — and each line seems to tip toward its own strokes, so the parallels appear to splay apart and together. it is a local tilt, summed by the eye. drop the slant to zero or the density to none, and the lines stand plainly parallel.
