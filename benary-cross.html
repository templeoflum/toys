the two gray triangles are the exact same gray. one sits on the arm of the cross, the other in the notch beside it — the same black and white around each. the one that reads as part of the cross looks lighter; the one that reads as sitting on the white background looks darker. lightness follows what a shape belongs to, not just its surround. fade the cross away and they match; or raise the bridge to join them with that same gray.
