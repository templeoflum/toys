a U-shaped tube holds a fluid, both arms open. press harder on the left arm and its column sinks by δ while the right rises by the same δ — the fluid is conserved, what leaves one arm enters the other. the two levels split by Δh = 2δ, and that height difference weighs exactly the pressure difference driving it: ΔP = ρg·Δh. the column's weight balances the push, so the height reads the pressure straight off the scale. dense mercury (ρ 13.6× water) splits far less for the same ΔP — a shorter, stiffer column.the U-tube manometer · fluid statics · ΔP = ρgΔh, the column weight balances the pressure · field: fluid statics / pressure measurement
