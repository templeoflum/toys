both gray bars are the exact same gray. the left one sits in a white stripe, the right one in a black stripe — and the left reads lighter, though it has more black around it. this runs backwards to plain contrast: the patch takes the lightness of the stripe it lies along, not its surround. fade the grating to a flat field, or raise the bridge to join the two with that same gray.
