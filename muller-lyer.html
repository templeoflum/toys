muller-lyer

fins
rule
 
the two black shafts are exactly the same length. the top wears out-turned fins (arrow-tails), the bottom in-turned ones (arrow-heads) — and the tails seem to pull the line longer, the heads to press it shorter. your eye reads the whole figure, not the shaft alone. shrink the fins to nothing and the two lines are plainly equal; pull the rule to drop guides from end to end.
