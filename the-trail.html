ants shuttle between the nest and the food. each ant drops pheromone on the cell it stands on and steps next toward the strongest scent nearby — with a dash of randomness so it still explores. pheromone evaporates everywhere every tick. two routes are open: a short one and a long one. no ant knows which is shorter, yet the colony converges on the short path — a round trip on it finishes sooner, so it gets more deposits per unit time, out-accumulating evaporation while the long path fades. a global path chosen by no one, built from local marks alone.stigmergy · ant-colony pheromone trails · Grassé 1959 · Deneubourg double-bridge 1990
